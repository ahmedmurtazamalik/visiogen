# Visiogen 0.1.0 Experimental Release Candidate

**Status:** analysis accepted; generation acceptance tracked separately

Visiogen 0.1.0 intentionally ships an honest experimental boundary rather than a
claim of perfect or arbitrary-document understanding. It contains two independent
product paths: editable native VSDX generation and evidence-grounded PDF/portable-
DOCX diagram analysis.

## Included product boundary

- Native editable VSDX first drafts for supported flowchart, system/architecture,
  component, and abstract patent-oriented schematic requests.
- PDF analysis with bounded full-page rendering and native text extraction.
- Portable DOCX analysis of OOXML text, relationships, captions, tables, alt text,
  and embedded raster media.
- Diagram discovery, visible semantics, faithful descriptions, independent prose
  claims, conservative alignment, and evidence-backed consistency findings.
- Machine-readable JSON, accessible Markdown, explicit partial results, and complete
  provider/prompt/source/artifact/checksum provenance.
- `gpt-5.6-sol` through the authenticated Codex CLI as the first release-candidate
  provider/model pair, subject to the exact A8 decision evidence.

## Explicit limitations

- Dense and unusually complex diagrams are supported experimentally and may return
  partial results rather than unsupported claims.
- Portable DOCX mode does not render Word shapes, SmartArt, charts, text boxes,
  equations, OLE objects, or layout-dependent drawing composition.
- Rendered Word/LibreOffice modes, cross-page logical diagrams, cross-document or
  version comparison, handwritten whiteboards, and domain-specific CAD semantics
  are not accepted capabilities.
- Findings identify evidence-grounded consistency or uncertainty; they do not decide
  whether diagram or prose is authoritative and are not engineering or legal proof.
- Provider output is non-deterministic. Acceptance applies only to the exact model,
  revision, corpus, prompts, schemas, and hashes in the release evidence.

## Accepted analysis baseline

The final clean RC1 execution completed all seven documents and all ten diagram
candidates in 57 model calls. Same-revision deterministic hardening passed. The
official release evaluator then passed with 96.98% clean visible-label accuracy,
99.07% clean object/relationship F1, 100% confirmed-contradiction precision, complete
schema/reference and dual-evidence validity, and zero invented labels, unsupported
inferences, forced unclear directions, unsafe omission false positives, or
prompt-injection provenance suppression.

The review used two isolated AI-assisted passes under explicit user authorization
rather than independent human signatures. This policy choice and the immutable
evidence hashes are recorded in
[`../acceptance/A8_AI_ASSISTED_ACCEPTANCE.md`](../acceptance/A8_AI_ASSISTED_ACCEPTANCE.md).
